What you can do here

  • Reorder — move any page earlier or later. Useful when a scanner fed the stack backwards.
  • Remove — drop the blank pages a scanner added, or the cover sheet a fax put on the front.
  • Duplicate — repeat a page, for instance when a form needs the same annexure twice.
  • Rotate — fix pages that came in sideways, one at a time or all at once.

Every change is provisional until you press save, so you can undo by pressing reset.

Why quality is not affected

Kagaz copies the underlying page objects into a new document rather than re-rendering them. Text stays selectable and searchable, vector graphics stay sharp at any zoom, and images are passed through untouched. The output is usually within a few kilobytes of the original.

Common questions

Is my file uploaded?

No. The thumbnails are rendered by pdf.js inside your browser, and the new PDF is assembled there too. There is no server.

Why is there no drag and drop?

Because dragging is miserable on a phone, and a lot of people reach for these tools on a phone. The arrow buttons work everywhere and with a keyboard. Adding drag as an extra on larger screens is a good first issue.

Can I combine two PDFs and then reorder?

Yes — merge them first, then bring the result here.
