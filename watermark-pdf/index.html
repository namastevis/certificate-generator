What a watermark is actually for

It is a social signal, not a lock. A watermark tells an honest reader that the copy in front of them is a draft, an unpaid proof, or not for circulation. It does not stop anyone determined — the mark is drawn on top of the page and can be removed by someone who wants to badly enough.

If you need to actually stop information getting out, remove it with Redact PDF rather than covering it up.

Getting it readable but not shouty

  • Opacity 15–30% is the sweet spot. Below 10% it vanishes on print; above 40% it fights the text.
  • 45° is conventional for a repeated mark because it crosses lines of text rather than running along them.
  • Repeating suits DRAFT or CONFIDENTIAL. A logo usually looks better once, in the centre.

Common questions

Is my file uploaded?

No. Both your PDF and your logo stay on your device. There is no server.

Can I put it behind the text instead of over it?

Not yet — the mark is drawn on top. Drawing underneath the existing content is a good first issue.

Can I remove a watermark from a PDF?

Not with this tool, and be wary of anything that promises it. Once stamped, the mark is ordinary page content and removing it means editing the document.
